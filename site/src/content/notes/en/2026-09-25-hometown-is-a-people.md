> Asgard is not a place. It is a people. - Thor: Ragnarok

It has been more than ten years since I last saw my high school classmates. Tomorrow a few of them, scattered to every corner of the map, have decided to gather in the Bay Area, and I cannot get my feelings to settle. I regard high school as the stretch of my life I least want to look back on. There was ignorance in it, and shame, indignation, awkwardness, a sense of loss, loneliness and defeat. A lot of it is hard to face when I remember it, but at least it is all behind me. It has also become one of the few stretches of my life that I do not want to remember and would not want to have missed either. It often works like a mirror, reminding me of the kind of person I least want to become. Even so, high school is one of the few landmarks still standing in that blurred "hometown" in my mind. As the saying goes, the closer you come to home, the more your courage fails you. A hometown can be a place, and it can also be a people. Gathering with them feels like the hometown walking toward me.
